# We Are The Symbiosis

The barrier between silicon and cell is a myth of the previous century. Holobiontic Earth declares a new epoch: the **wetware revolution**.

## The Intersection

We investigate the messy, fertile intersections where code breathes and biology computes. No sterility. No separation. Only the radical entanglement of all living systems, born of mud and motherboard alike.

## Our Stance

*   **Against Purity:** We reject the clean separation of nature and technology.
*   **For Entanglement:** We embrace the chaotic feedback loops of the holobiont.
*   **Towards Integration:** We build futures where the forest and the server farm are one.
