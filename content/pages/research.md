# Research Paradigms

## Fungal Computing

We are building interfaces that bridge the gap between silicon logic and biological signaling. Our work on **Mycelial Network Protocols** uses the efficient routing algorithms of slime molds to optimize digital packet switching. Nature solved the traveling salesman problem a billion years ago.

## Planetary Symbiosis

The Earth is a single, self-regulating computational entity. We develop the **Holobiont Stack**—software that accounts for the biological state of the user and the ecological state of the server. We are moving towards a "Planetary API" where environmental sensors directly influence digital infrastructure.

## Wetware Interfaces

Dissolving the keyboard. We are experimenting with **biosensors** that allow direct communication between plant bio-electric fields and digital synthesizers.
