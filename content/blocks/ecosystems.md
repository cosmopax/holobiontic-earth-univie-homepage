# Ecosystems in Focus

We work across diverse habitats to understand symbiotic coordination and resilience.

- **Riparian systems:** microbial, plant, and animal co-evolution along river networks.
- **Urban ecologies:** multi-species collaboration in shared human spaces.
- **Soil intelligence:** subterranean networks, fungi, and nutrient exchange.
- **Coastal margins:** adaptive interfaces between land, sea, and atmospheric flows.
