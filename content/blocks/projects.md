# Symbiotic Programs

Our programs translate planetary-scale questions into fieldwork, prototypes, and living systems experiments. Each program blends sensing, modeling, and stewardship to cultivate durable ecological futures.

- **Planetary interfaces:** tools that reveal Earth-system feedback in real time.
- **Ecological modeling:** multi-scale simulations of symbiosis and resilience.
- **Symbiotic infrastructures:** experiments that bridge biology and computation.
